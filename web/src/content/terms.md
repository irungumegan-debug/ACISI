## Part 1: For everyone

### What ACISI is, and what it is not

ACISI is an online records and administration system for clinics. It handles patient check-in and queues, visit records and prescriptions, payments, receipts and SMS visit summaries.

ACISI is not a healthcare provider. We do not diagnose, treat or give medical advice, and nothing in ACISI replaces advice from a qualified health worker. All medical care and decisions come from the clinic and its staff.

**In an emergency, do not use ACISI.** Go straight to the nearest hospital or call 999 or 112.

### Acceptable use

Please use ACISI honestly and only for its purpose. Do not use someone else's details or account without permission, give false information, or make check-ins you do not intend to use. Do not try to get into records you are not entitled to see, break or overload the system, or get around its security. Do not use ACISI in any way that breaks Kenyan law. We may suspend accounts that are misused, and report serious misuse to the authorities.

### Privacy

How we collect, use, protect and keep personal data, and your rights over it, are set out in our Privacy Notice at acisi.co.ke/privacy. All patient data is stored and processed in Kenya.

### Availability

We work to keep ACISI running and secure at all times. It may occasionally be unavailable, for example during maintenance, which we schedule outside clinic hours where possible, or if power, internet, M-Pesa or SMS services fail. If ACISI is down, clinics continue with their usual paper process, and patients can still be checked in at the front desk.

Where the law allows, our total responsibility is limited to what you paid us in the 12 months before the problem arose: for patients, the check-in fees; for clinics, the subscription and setup fees. Nothing in these terms takes away rights you have under the Consumer Protection Act or any other Kenyan law that cannot be limited.

## Part 2: For patients

### Using ACISI

You can use ACISI for yourself if you are 18 or older. A parent or guardian checks in and manages records for a child under 18. Please give accurate details, such as your name, phone number and ID number, and update them if they change, so your records are not mixed up or missed.

You log in to the patient portal with your PIN. If you forget it, you can reset it with a one-time code sent to your phone. Keep your PIN and phone to yourself, and tell us straight away if you think someone else has used your account.

### Checking in and the queue

When you check in, you choose the clinic and department, and ACISI places you in that clinic's queue. Any waiting time or position we show is an estimate. The clinic decides the order patients are seen, and may see urgent cases first. A check-in does not guarantee you will be seen that day: the clinic may close early, a doctor may be unavailable, or the clinic may refer you elsewhere.

### The check-in fee

Checking in remotely costs KSh 100, paid to ACISI by M-Pesa when you check in. The fee pays for the check-in service and is not part of your medical bill. Walk-in patients checked in by clinic staff do not pay it. If the fee ever changes, we will show the new amount before you pay.

Your bill for the visit itself is set by the clinic and paid to the clinic's own till, paybill, card machine or cash desk, never to ACISI. Questions about that bill go to the clinic.

### Refunds

We refund the KSh 100 check-in fee in full if the clinic cancels, closes or cannot see you on the day you checked in, if M-Pesa charged you more than once for the same check-in, or if you were charged but the check-in did not go through. We do not refund the fee if you do not arrive, or if you leave before being seen.

To ask for a refund, email acisi.help@gmail.com or call 0746404155 within 14 days, with your phone number and the M-Pesa reference. We send approved refunds back to the same M-Pesa number within 7 days.

### Messages and your records

We send SMS that are part of your care: check-in confirmations, receipts, visit summaries and appointment reminders. Visit summaries never include your diagnosis. You can stop them at any time through the clinic staff or the setting in your patient portal.

The clinic is responsible for your medical records and decides what goes into them. Another clinic can see your history only with your explicit consent.

### Closing your account

You can stop using ACISI at any time. To close your patient portal account, contact us. The clinic may still need to keep your visit records for the periods set out in our Privacy Notice.

## Part 3: For clinics

### Plans, fees and payment

The clinic chooses a plan when it signs up. The plan sets the monthly or yearly price, how many doctors and departments are included, and how many SMS are included each month. Extra SMS beyond the allowance are charged at the rate in the plan.

A one-time setup fee covers setting up the clinic's departments, fees, staff accounts and payment details, and training staff. It is paid before setup begins, and is not refunded once training has taken place.

Subscription fees are paid in advance, monthly or yearly, by M-Pesa or bank transfer. We send an invoice before each period, due within 7 days. If a payment is more than 14 days late, we will remind the clinic and may then pause access until it is paid. We never delete the clinic's records because of late payment.

Founding clinics receive the discounted price and conditions set out in their founding clinic offer, locked for the period stated there. After that period, the clinic moves to the standard price, with at least 30 days' notice. We may change our prices with at least 30 days' written notice, and a new price applies from the clinic's next billing period.

The patient check-in fee is charged by ACISI to patients who check in remotely, and is separate from the clinic's subscription. Payments for patients' visits go to the clinic's own till, paybill or card machine, never to ACISI.

### The clinic's responsibilities

The clinic is responsible for the accuracy of what its staff enter into ACISI, and for all medical decisions and care. It must hold the licences and registrations it needs to operate as a health facility in Kenya.

The clinic manages its own staff accounts: it gives each staff member their own login, keeps logins confidential, gives each person only the access their role needs, and removes access promptly when someone leaves. The clinic is responsible for what is done under its staff accounts.

As data controller for its patients' records, the clinic makes sure patients are told how their data is used, records the consents the law requires, and uses patient data only for providing care and running the clinic. It must not use ACISI to send marketing messages containing health information.

The clinic provides its own internet connection and devices, and its own M-Pesa till or paybill and card machine for patient payments.

### Our responsibilities and support

We host ACISI on servers in Kenya, keep it secure and up to date, make nightly encrypted backups, and fix problems as quickly as we reasonably can.

We set up the clinic and train its staff as part of the setup fee, and give support by phone, WhatsApp or email during working hours. During the first weeks of a founding clinic's use, we also support staff on site.

### Patient data

The clinic is the data controller for its patients' records, and ACISI is the data processor. We process those records only to provide ACISI to the clinic, and only on the clinic's instructions. Before any real patient data is entered, the clinic and ACISI sign a Data Processing Agreement setting out these duties in detail, as Kenya's Data Protection Act requires.

The clinic's patient records belong to the clinic. We never sell them, use them for advertising, or share them with anyone except as the clinic instructs, as the patient consents, or as the law requires. ACISI and everyone working for us keep them confidential, during and after our agreement. If we become aware of a breach affecting the clinic's data, we tell the clinic within 48 hours and help it meet its own duty to notify the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner.

We may use anonymous statistics that cannot identify any patient or clinic, such as the total number of check-ins across ACISI, to run and improve the service.

### Ending the service and getting your data back

The clinic may end the service at any time with 30 days' written notice. Fees already paid for the current period are not refunded, except that yearly plans ended early are refunded for any full months left after the notice period.

We may end the service with 30 days' written notice, or sooner if the clinic seriously breaks these terms, for example by not paying for more than 30 days after a reminder, or by misusing patient data, and does not put it right within 14 days of being told.

When the service ends, the clinic can export all of its records free of charge, in a common format, for 30 days. After that, we delete the clinic's data from ACISI, and from our backups as they roll over, except anything the law requires us to keep. The clinic's data is always its own, and we will never hold it back.
