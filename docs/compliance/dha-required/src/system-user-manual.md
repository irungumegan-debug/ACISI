## 1. About ACISI

ACISI is a web-based clinic management system for outpatient clinics in Kenya. It works in any modern web browser on a phone, tablet or computer. Nothing needs to be installed.

- Staff use the console at **acisi.co.ke/console**.
- Patients use the patient portal at **acisi.co.ke**.

## 2. Getting started for staff

### Logging in

1. Open acisi.co.ke/console.
2. Enter your staff ID and your 6-digit PIN.
3. Clinic administrators and doctors also enter the 6-digit code from their authenticator app.

After 5 wrong PINs your account locks for 5 minutes. You are logged out after 15 minutes without activity.

### Forgotten PIN

Choose **Forgot PIN** on the login page and follow the steps, or ask your clinic administrator to reset it.

### What you see

The menu shows only what your role needs:

| Role | Menu |
|---|---|
| Front desk | Queue, Patients, Appointments |
| Clinician | Queue, Patients, Appointments, National HIE |
| Doctor | My queue, Appointments, Disease surveillance, Routine reports, National HIE |
| Lab technician | Lab queue |
| Clinic administrator | Queue, Patients, Appointments, Daily summary, Routine reports, Disease surveillance, National HIE, Settings |

## 3. Front desk

- **Register a patient:** Queue, then walk-in. Enter name and phone; other details are optional. The patient gets an ACISI ID.
- **Check in:** add the patient to the right department queue. Patients can also check in themselves on the portal.
- **Record vital signs:** open the visit and enter the readings. Warnings show for values out of range.
- **Patients:** search by name or phone to see or correct details.
- **Appointments:** book, view and check in booked patients.
- **Checkout:** when the doctor has finished, open the visit, add items, record the payment by cash or M-Pesa, mark medicines given, and print or send the receipt.

## 4. Doctors

1. Open **My queue** and select the next patient.
2. Review history, allergies, problems and vital signs.
3. Record the consultation notes.
4. Choose 1 to 5 ICD-11 diagnoses.
5. Prescribe from the medicine list. A warning shows if the patient is allergic.
6. Order lab tests and other services if needed.
7. Update the problem list and family history.
8. Sign with your PIN. Signed notes cannot be changed.

If a diagnosis is a notifiable disease, an alert shows. Report it to the Sub-County and mark it reported under **Disease surveillance**.

## 5. Lab technicians

1. Open **Lab queue** and select a request.
2. Enter the results.
3. Sign with your PIN.

Critical results alert the requesting doctor at once.

## 6. Clinic administrators

- **Settings:** add and deactivate staff, manage departments, lab tests and payment settings, and the Sub-County reporting contact.
- **Daily summary:** visits and payments for the day.
- **Routine reports:** monthly, quarterly and annual reports and quality measures, without patient names.
- **Disease surveillance:** notifiable disease counts and the weekly MOH 505 summary.
- **National HIE:** the clinic's link to the national health information exchange, once DHA provides access.

Deactivate staff who leave on the day they leave.

## 7. Patients

- **Sign up** at acisi.co.ke with your phone number. You must be 18 or older; children are registered at the clinic with a guardian.
- **Check in** from home and pay the check-in fee by M-Pesa.
- **My records:** see and download your visits, medicines and lab results.
- **Sharing:** choose whether other clinics may see your records. Sharing is off until you allow it.
- **Files:** upload photos or scans of your own records.
- **Delete my account:** on your patient home page.
